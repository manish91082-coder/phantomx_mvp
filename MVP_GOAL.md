# MVP GOAL SPECIFICATION

## Mission outcome
PHANTOMX must progress toward a real executable arbitrage opportunity, but only through independently verified gates.

## Hard requirements
- Zero mandatory paid infrastructure.
- Serverless-first operation.
- Fast path built around async I/O and deterministic filtering.
- AI-assisted multi-agent proposal layer.
- Deterministic verification as final decision authority.
- Atomic execution.
- Fail-closed safety.
- No fixed pair, spread, gas, loan amount, fee, or synthetic profitability assumption in the final hot path.
- Conservative net profit strictly greater than USD 0.50 after all known costs and risk buffers.
- Realized profit only after receipt and independent wallet reconciliation.

## Definition of done
Done is a proof state, not a code state. The final goal gate remains RED until all required evidence is present.
